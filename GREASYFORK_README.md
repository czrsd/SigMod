# Sigmally Mod | SigMod Client 🚀

**SigMod** is the ultimate free userscript and mod for **Sigmally.com**. It enhances the game with powerful macros, a live party minimap, custom themes, skins, and advanced quality-of-life features.

> **Note:** Sigmally is an Agar.io private server. SigMod is developed exclusively for Sigmally and does not work on the official Agar.io game.

---

## ✨ Features at a Glance

### ⚡ Advanced Sigmally Macros
Take full control of your gameplay with lightning-fast keyboard and mouse macros:
- **Rapid Feed:** Adjustable auto-feed speed for perfect teaming.
- **Split Mechanics:** Double split, Triple split, Quad split, and Instant splits.
- **Trick Splits:** Double Trick, Self Trick, and Line splits (Horizontal, Vertical, Fixed).
- **Quality of Life:** Auto/Instant Respawn hotkeys and configurable mouse button macros.

### 🗺️ Party Minimap & Social Features
Never lose your teammates again! Playing with other SigMod users unlocks exclusive party mechanics:
- **Live Party Minimap:** See your friends' exact positions in real-time.
- **Map Pings & Location Sharing:** Coordinate attacks and retreats instantly.
- **Party Chat & Panel:** Communicate securely with your tag without leaving the game.
- **Friends System:** Send friend requests, view online status, check profiles, and send private messages.

### 🎨 Custom Skins, Themes & Visuals
Completely overhaul the Sigmally client to match your style:
- **Custom Graphics:** Modify cell colors, food colors, borders, maps, and viruses.
- **Skins & Text:** Upload custom skin replacements, gradient names, custom fonts, and name colors.
- **UI Themes:** Choose from built-in preset themes or build your own with custom backgrounds and gradients.
- **Menu Customization:** Fully style the Sigmally menu and input fields.

### 🛠️ Extra Utilities
- **Daily Rewards:** Automatic daily coin claiming.
- **Gameplay Stats:** Track your performance with the play timer and gameplay statistics.
- **Media Tools:** Image capture, gallery, and saved image downloads.
- **Quick Access Menu:** Search for settings, keybindings, and tools instantly.

## ⚙️ How to Use SigMod

Once installed, you can open the extensive SigMod configuration menu by:
- Pressing the **`V`** key on your keyboard.
- Clicking the **gear icon** ⚙️ on the main Sigmally screen.

From here, you can customize your macros, visual settings, name presets, themes, gallery, and friend list.
