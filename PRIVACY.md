# Privacy

SigMod is a browser mod for Sigmally. This page explains what information we use, why we use it, and what choices you have.

## What stays in your browser

SigMod saves your settings locally, including key and mouse bindings, language, appearance, themes, chat and party preferences, and feature choices. Some settings contain values you choose, such as a party tag or image URL. Settings backups are files you export yourself.

Match history is also stored locally in your browser. It can include match time and duration, highest mass, kills, splits, rank, leaderboard time, ping, player counts, and party size. We do not send this match history to SigMod's server.

The browser also stores items such as dismissed alerts, recent tabs or emojis, and account sign-in tokens. Anyone with access to your browser profile may be able to read its stored data.

## Information we receive when you use online features

When you connect to SigMod's online services, we process the information needed to provide the feature you're using. This can include your game nickname, server and party tag, client version, connection and account IDs, party membership, score, skin and color, alive status, position, and ping.

When Sigmally sends us your authenticated game profile, we store the account ID and profile information provided, which can include your email address, Google ID, displayed name, and nickname. We also store the connection IP address and details derived from your browser's user-agent, such as browser and platform. We remove the Google token before saving the profile.

If you create or use a SigMod account, we store its username, password hash or Discord ID, avatar, role, bio, badges, visibility and online status, timestamps, and profile preferences. Passwords are hashed. Friends and friend requests are stored with the related account IDs.

Private messages are stored with their text, sender, recipient, and timestamp so you and the other participant can access chat history. Party chat messages are sent to connected members of the party, but we do not save their text as part of party statistics.

## Analytics and party statistics

The **Analytics** setting is on by default. You can switch it off in SigMod settings. While it is on, we receive selected settings and updates when they change. These include language, macro and keybind choices, game appearance and behavior preferences, theme and party-panel options, and chat display settings. Some selected values can contain user-entered text or URLs, such as your party tag or image settings.

We also count selected keybind activations and changes. Reports include the action, input type, configured binding, and a count. They are sent in batches and do not contain a time-stamped replay of each key press.

Analytics reports use a random installation ID that stays with the browser profile. It is not your name, but it lets us connect reports from the same installation over time, so it is pseudonymous rather than anonymous. Switching Analytics off stops future settings and keybind reports from that installation and clears pending reports in that browser. It does not delete information already received.

We collect daily party totals for sessions, joins, leaves, reconnects, chat-message counts, durations, and party-size groups. We do not store player names or party tags in these daily records, and the chat-message count does not include message text. When Analytics is on, your saved party tag may still be included in the settings snapshot described above. Party totals are collected on the server and have no user-facing opt-out.

## How we use account information

We use account and gameplay information to provide sign-in, profiles, friends, chat, party features, support, and to protect and operate SigMod. Authorized SigMod administrators may look up relevant account information when handling support requests, investigating security or moderation issues, or managing community activities. This can include checking giveaway eligibility or contacting a selected winner. Account lookups are restricted to authorized administrators; they are not a public search service.

Public profile listings can show your username, avatar, role, bio, badges, and online status. Profiles marked hidden may still appear in username search results. Profile visibility does not hide your information from SigMod administrators.

If an administrator requests the approximate location of a public IP address associated with an account, we send that IP to IPWho.is for lookup. Private and reserved IP addresses are not sent. We cache successful lookups in memory for up to 24 hours.

## Services that receive information

We do not sell personal information. We send information to other services only where needed for a feature or request:

- **Discord** handles SigMod account and administrator sign-in and may host profile avatars.
- **Sigmally** handles the game and its account system. If daily challenges are enabled for your signed-in game account, we send your email address to Sigmally's challenge service.
- **IPWho.is** receives a public IP only when an administrator requests its approximate location.
- **Google Fonts, jsDelivr, unpkg, and image hosts** may receive a browser request when a font, library, or image is loaded. If you use a custom image or font URL, the browser contacts that host directly.

These services receive the connection information needed to handle requests, such as an IP address and request metadata. Their own privacy practices are separate from ours.

## Retention

- Party statistics and keybind usage records are configured to expire after 180 days.
- Settings snapshots, account records, friend records, and private messages have no automatic expiry configured.
- IP geolocation results are cached in memory for up to 24 hours.
- Information stored only in your browser remains there until you clear it or remove it yourself.

## Your choices

- Turn **Analytics** off to stop future settings and keybind reports from this browser. This does not remove reports already received.
- Use profile visibility and friend-request settings to control profile listing and incoming requests.
- You can use SigMod without a SigMod account, but account features such as friends, profiles, and direct messages require one.
- Online features need to send the relevant information to SigMod's services to work. There is no user setting to turn off the server-side party totals.

For account support and privacy requests such as data export or deletion, create a ticket on the [SigMod Discord](https://discord.gg/RjxeZ2eRGg) or write a DM to `czrsd` on Discord.
